## 🧭 What I Do

**Legal RAG, end to end.** I lead the build of *DEVON AI*, a B2B legal assistant
for law firms and corporate legal teams — custom RAG pipelines in Node.js over
**2M+ indexed legal documents**: statutes, compliance frameworks, court judgments.
Also the unglamorous wins: cut cost-per-query by right-sizing retrieval context
and adding caching, and onboarded 20+ lawyers and legal departments.

**Anti-bot distributed scraping.** Three years building a high-performance
distributed scraping service in Python and Node.js, against continuously evolving
WAFs and bot protections — Cloudflare, Incapsula, Akamai, PerimeterX, DataDome.
The place where you learn resilience, backoff and fallback the hard way: on
infrastructure that actively wants you to fail.

**Radios & reverse-engineering.** For my B.Sc. thesis I built a two-part BLE
capture & decode toolkit — an aggregator for multiple nRF dongles sniffing all
three advertising channels in real time, plus an SDR module on a **HackRF** to
demodulate and decode arbitrary BLE packets. Most AI engineers have never touched
an SDR; I like problems that reach down to the radio layer.

*CS undergrad → technical co-founder → national cybersecurity finalist, all in the
same stretch of years. I move fast across stacks that don't usually talk to each other.*
